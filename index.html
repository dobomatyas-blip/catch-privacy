Catch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Catch ("the app") is a note-taking app for iPhone and Mac, made by Endless Solutions Kft. ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: your notes are yours, they live on your devices and in your own iCloud Drive, and we do not run servers that store them.

1. Your notes stay in your own storage

  • Notes, feelings, times and places you record are stored on your device and in a folder of your own iCloud Drive that you choose. We (the developer) have no access to this folder.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your notes with anyone, and we do not show ads.

2. AI features (only if you turn them on)

Catch can sort a note (its type and time), write a daily, weekly or monthly summary, and similar features. For these, the app sends data directly from your device to the AI provider you set up, using your own API key:

  • Anthropic (api.anthropic.com), and optionally xAI (api.x.ai) or DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com).
  • What is sent: the text of the notes involved, and for summaries also the time, the place name and the feeling word attached to those notes.
  • We do not receive or store this data. Each provider processes it under its own privacy terms. You can turn AI features off in Settings, or remove your API key at any time.

3. Location

If you allow it, Catch saves where a note was made (an approximate place) together with the note in your storage. It can be included in AI requests for summaries (see section 2). You can turn this off in Settings › Capture, or in iOS Settings.

4. Apple Health (read-only)

If you turn it on, Catch reads sleep, steps, exercise and daylight (and mindful minutes) to show them on your day and month pages. Health data is processed only on your iPhone: it is never sent to an AI provider, to analytics, to iCloud, or to us. Catch never writes to Apple Health.

5. Anonymous usage analytics

To improve the app, Catch sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (EU servers, eu.i.posthog.com), for example "app opened", "note saved", "task ticked" — with numbers only (such as how long something took or how many characters a note has) and a random installation ID.

  • Never sent: the text or title of any note, feelings, places, Health data, names or contacts.
  • No personal profile is created, the data is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • You can turn it off at any time: Settings › Share anonymous usage.

6. Delivery to other apps (Mac)

If you use the Mac companion, notes can be delivered to apps on your own Mac (for example Calendar, OmniFocus or Drafts). This happens locally on your computer, through your own iCloud Drive folder.

7. Children

Catch is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your choices

Everything above can be switched off in the app: AI features, location, Apple Health and analytics. Deleting the app and its iCloud Drive folder removes your notes; anonymous analytics events cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be individually retrieved.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the way Catch handles data changes; the date at the top shows the latest version.
